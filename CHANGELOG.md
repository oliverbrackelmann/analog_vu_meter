## 0.1.0

* First version.
* `VuMeter` with six faces (`neon`, `classic`, `backlit`, `studio`, `fan`,
  `paper`), clip lamp, optional label, percent scale and needle ballistics.
* `VuScale`: analog (−20 … +6 dB), digital (−40 … 0 dBFS) and custom scales.
* Any shape from 1 : 1 to 2 : 1 (`aspectRatio`), laid out anew instead of
  stretched.
* `VuMeterBezel` with four frames (`brushed`, `silver`, `black`, `acrylic`).
